## Q0 — Inventory of 21 distinct opportunities (who pays, demand, economics, AI rules, automation, capital, time, failure, verdict)

### Takeaway
Of 21 models reviewed, none shows verified, hands-off profitability for a new entrant relying on bulk AI output; the ones with defensible evidence (paid niche B2B/finance newsletters, niche job boards/directories with proprietary data, Etsy "designed by seller" digital goods, Udemy/own courses in an expertise niche) all depend on a scarce human input (expertise, data, distribution) with AI doing production. Pure AI-volume plays (AI content sites, thin affiliate, templated faceless YouTube, bulk KDP, AI stock, AI podcasts) are either policy-targeted or structurally saturated.

## Q1 — What happened to AI-generated content sites and affiliate SEO in 2024–2026?

### Takeaway
Two compounding shocks: (1) Google policy/algorithm enforcement (Helpful Content 2023, March 2024 "scaled content abuse" / site-reputation / expired-domain policies, repeated 2026 spam and core updates) and (2) AI Overviews/AI Mode cutting click-through on informational queries by roughly 35–60% in most studies. Small publishers lost far more search traffic than large ones.

### Cited Findings
- Google's spam policy defines scaled content abuse as "many pages are generated for the primary purpose of manipulating search rankings" and lists using generative AI to mass-produce pages without user value, scraping/transforming feeds, stitching content, and spreading content over multiple sites; it also defines site reputation abuse, expired domain abuse and thin affiliation; spam now includes attempts to manipulate "generative AI responses in Google Search" — PRIMARY [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).
- The policy is method-neutral (human, AI or mixed) — SECONDARY [Practical Ecommerce](https://www.practicalecommerce.com/google-spam-policies-explained).
- 2026 spam updates reported in June, August and September 2026 (the last starting 24 Sep 2026, scaled content abuse in scope); after June 2026, declines were observed on sites Ahrefs flags as "Very high" AI content (correlation) — SECONDARY [AIOSEO](https://aioseo.com/google-june-2026-spam-update/), [CMSWire](https://www.cmswire.com/digital-experience/googles-new-ai-spam-detector-judges-networks-not-pages-high-ai-sites-are-already-slipping/), [Omnius](https://www.omnius.so/blog/google-spam-update).
- Ahrefs (Feb 2026, 300,000 keywords): position-1 CTR −58% when an AI Overview appears (was −34.5% in its 2025 study); position-1 CTR on AIO keywords 7.3% (Mar 2024) → 2.6% (Mar 2025) → 1.6% (Dec 2025) — SECONDARY (unverified mapping) [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-ai-overviews-impact-on-publishers-how-to-adapt-into-2026/556843/).
- Pew Research (2025, observed panel): users clicked a traditional result on 8% of visits with an AI summary vs 15% without — SECONDARY [VideoWeek, 23 Jul 2025](https://videoweek.com/2025/07/23/pew-research-demonstrates-sharp-impact-of-ai-overviews-on-publisher-search-traffic/).
- Seer Interactive (Nov 2025): organic CTR on informational queries with AIO −61% since mid-2024; paid CTR −68% — SECONDARY [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-ai-overviews-reduce-organic-ctr-61-paid-traffic-68/).
- Academic study (Agarwal & Sen): AIO reduced outbound organic clicks by 39.8%, zero-click searches +34.5% (preprint) — SECONDARY [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/researchers-find-google-ai-overviews-cut-publisher-clicks-39-8/).
- Chartbeat/Reuters Institute: Google search referrals to publishers ~−33/34% globally; small publishers −60%, mid −47%, large −22% — SECONDARY (unverified mapping) [DesignRush](https://news.designrush.com/ai-overviews-publisher-traffic-website-strategy-2026), [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/googles-ai-search-features-cut-publisher-traffic-data-from-s-d1c19012).
- Counterpoint: Google states total organic click volume is "relatively stable" year over year; Amsive finds branded queries with AIO get +18% CTR — SECONDARY (search summary) [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-ai-overviews-impact-on-publishers-how-to-adapt-into-2026/556843/).
- Niche/affiliate casualties: HouseFresh −91–95% Google traffic after Sept 2023 HCU; Teitelman survey: nearly half of surveyed niche/affiliate sites lost ≥91% (Dec 2023–Aug 2024) — SECONDARY [Crowdo](https://crowdo.net/blog/helpful-content-update-traffic-loss-2025/).
- World History Encyclopedia reported −25% traffic in one month after AIO inclusion — SECONDARY [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/as-ai-takes-his-readers-this-leading-history-publisher-wonders-whats-next).
- Trade press notes winners emerging among affiliates that diversify away from search — SECONDARY [Hello Partner, 26 May 2026](https://hellopartner.com/2026/05/26/as-the-new-ai-reality-bites-affiliates-winners-are-already-emerging/).

### Inferences
- The informational, definition-style, "best X" content that AI can produce cheaply is exactly the content AI Overviews answer on-SERP; the supply shock and demand shock hit the same pages.

## Q2 — Platform policies on AI-generated content (YouTube, KDP, Etsy, Medium, Substack, Amazon Associates)

### Takeaway

- Spotify (podcasts): May 2026 ban on unauthorised voice-clone impersonation, verified badges — SECONDARY [Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/spotify-extends-verified-by-spotify-badges-to-podcasts-further-cracking-down-on-ai-impersonators/).

- The common test across platforms is "human creative input per unit", not "was AI used". An agent-run operation must be designed so that each unit has distinct research/design, and disclosure is made where required (KDP, Etsy).

## Q6 — Which models still show defensible, verified profitability for new entrants, and which are dead or spam/policy-dominated?

### Takeaway
Verified evidence of new-entrant profitability exists only where there is owned distribution plus scarce input: paid niche newsletters in high-value verticals, a minority of job boards/directories, and expertise-based digital products. ### Cited Findings
- Still showing growth with first-party data: paid newsletters (+138% 2025 on beehiiv; investing LTV $230 median) — PRIMARY [beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/the-state-of-paid-newsletters-2026).

- Demand-eroded: search-dependent publishing (small publishers −60% search referrals) — SECONDARY [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/googles-ai-search-features-cut-publisher-traffic-data-from-s-d1c19012); stock assets (Adobe stock line decline) — SECONDARY [Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/730560/adbe-q1-deep-dive-ai-transition-ceo-succession-and-freemium-push-shape-outlook); affiliate commissions (Amazon cuts May 2026, single-source) — SECONDARY [novadata](https://novadata.io/resources/news/amazon-associates-commission-cut-may-2026).

### Inferences
Classification as of Nov 2026 (my synthesis):
- DEFENSIBLE WITH A REAL EDGE (not passive): niche B2B/finance paid briefing; data-monitoring alert product; niche job board or directory with proprietary/structured data and non-Google distribution; expertise course (Udemy for demand, own site for margin).
- MARGINAL / LOTTERY-LIKE: Etsy digital downloads and POD with original designs (accessible from Morocco, policy tightening); researched educational faceless YouTube; Notion/Canva templates with an audience; KDP with disclosure and real editorial value.
- EFFECTIVELY DEAD OR POLICY-DOMINATED FOR NEW ENTRANTS: bulk AI content sites; thin Amazon affiliate SEO; low-value programmatic SEO; templated faceless YouTube and Shorts farms; AI stock assets; bulk AI KDP books; Medium Partner Program with AI text; AI podcast farms; ad-funded dropshipping from Morocco.
- For a time-poor owner, the honest expected outcome of any single model is near the median (tens of dollars/month); the brief's "minimal personal time" conflicts with the fact that every defensible model's scarce input is human (expertise, relationships, editorial judgment, sales).

### Gaps

- Morocco-specific demand (French/Arabic/Darija niches, local B2B information products) was not researched and may be the least saturated angle.
