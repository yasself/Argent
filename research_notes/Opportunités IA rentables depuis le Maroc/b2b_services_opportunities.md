## Key Question 1 — Which productized B2B AI services have verifiable paying demand and published prices in 2025-2026? (Inventory of 24 models)

### Takeaway
Paying demand with public prices is verifiable in almost every category asked about; the strongest operator-published revenue evidence is in outbound/lead-gen tooling, AI SEO/AEO content, and support chatbots, while voice receptionists, price monitoring, document parsing and localization show mature, low published price points (USD 30-800/month). Evidence of revenue for *solo, hands-off* operators specifically is thin: most verified figures belong to product companies with teams, not to one-person "agent-run" services.

## Key Question 2 — What do incumbents charge, and where are markets saturated or dominated by funded players?

### Takeaway
Self-serve SMB categories have converged on low anchors (voice USD 30-300/mo, chatbots USD 40-500/mo, review replies USD 10-44/location, website translation from EUR 15/mo, SEO articles about USD 3 each), which leaves little room for an undifferentiated reseller. Higher prices (USD 900-5,000/mo, USD 10-20k/yr) survive only in sales-led categories: AI SDR, RFP, compliance.

### Cited Findings
- AI receptionists cluster at USD 30-300/mo for most SMBs; human-staffed services USD 129-1,400/mo (secondary) — [OnceHub](https://www.oncehub.com/blog/answering-service-cost), [Dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/how-much-does-an-ai-receptionist-cost)
- Support automation is priced per result by the category leader: USD 0.99 per Fin outcome (fetched 2026-11-11) — [Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/pricing)
- AI SEO content is priced at USD 99 for 30 articles (fetched 2026-11-11) — [Outrank](https://www.outrank.so/pricing)
- GEO tracking entry tiers are near-identical per tracked prompt: Peec USD 1.90, Otterly USD 1.93, Profound USD 1.98 (secondary analysis) — [Ryze](https://www.get-ryze.ai/blog/ai-visibility-tools-pricing-compared-2026)
- AI SDR: 11x ~USD 5,000/mo (secondary) versus AiSDR USD 1.50 per prospect self-serve (fetched) — [Landbase](https://www.landbase.com/blog/11x-ai-pricing), [AiSDR](https://aisdr.com/pricing/)
- Compliance/RFP tier: Conveyor USD 9,600/yr, Vanta ~USD 10-20k/yr, AutoRFP USD 899-1,299/mo (all secondary) — [Wolfia on Conveyor](https://connect.wolfia.com/blog/conveyor-reviews-pricing-alternatives), [Wolfia on Vanta](https://connect.wolfia.com/blog/vanta-reviews-pricing-alternatives), [AutoRFP](https://autorfp.ai/blog/loopio-pricing)
- Gartner warned of "agent washing" and estimated only about 130 of the thousands of agentic-AI vendors are real (June 2025; read via coverage, not the release itself) — [Gartner press release](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-predicts-over-40-percent-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-canceled-by-end-of-2027)

### Inferences
- Most saturated for a new solo entrant: English-language AI receptionists, generic website chatbots, AI SEO article generators, GEO trackers, AI SDR tools.
- Least commoditised by price, but hardest to sell without calls and credibility: RFP, security questionnaires, managed outbound.
- Where incumbents price per unit of outcome (Intercom), a reseller cannot compete on software; only on setup, niche knowledge or language.

### Gaps
- No funding totals or market-share data were collected per category; "dominated by funded players" rests on pricing behaviour and named incumbents, not on share figures.
- a16z, Sequoia and McKinsey reports were not consulted.

## Key Question 3 — Which can be sold WITHOUT sales calls versus needing human selling?

### Takeaway
Marketplace and self-serve models (Apify actors, usage-based APIs, low-ticket SaaS-like subscriptions under roughly USD 300/mo) are sold without calls by the incumbents themselves; anything managed, enterprise or above ~USD 1,000/mo is visibly sold through "book a call". The no-call models are also the ones with the lowest prices.

### Cited Findings
- Marketplace distribution with built-in billing: Apify Store handles payouts monthly; developer publishes and earns on usage — [Apify docs](https://docs.apify.com/actors/publishing/monetize/monthly-payouts.md)

### Inferences
- Ranking by "no human selling" fit: M6 (marketplace) > M5, M9, M15, M17 (API/self-serve) > M16, M18, M19, M13 (self-serve but need content/SEO distribution) > M1, M7 (self-serve possible, SMBs often want a demo) > M3, M10, M11, M12, M14, M23 (calls, trust and onboarding required).
- For local SMBs in Morocco and francophone Africa, purchase by phone/WhatsApp and in-person trust is likely the norm, which works against a no-call model there. This is my assumption; I found no data.

### Gaps
- No conversion-rate or CAC data for self-serve versus sales-led in these categories.
- Malt, Fiverr and ComeUp category demand data were not obtained beyond two individual ComeUp gig pages.

## Key Question 4 — Which niches are under-served for francophone markets or SMEs in Africa/Europe?

### Takeaway
I found no hard evidence of an *unserved* francophone niche: French-language AI receptionists, tender-monitoring tools and e-invoicing platforms already exist with public or free pricing. The plausible gaps are narrower — Morocco/Darija/Arabic-French voice and support, Moroccan and African public tenders, and French e-invoicing emission prep before September 2027 — and each is an inference, not a demonstrated demand.

### Cited Findings
- France already has priced AI switchboards: Eliocall from EUR 79 HT/mo for 150 minutes; Fonio 1,000 minutes from EUR 99 HT/mo billed annually; market range EUR 59-400 HT/mo (secondary comparisons) — [La Fabrique du Net](https://www.lafabriquedunet.fr/logiciels/tech/standard-telephonique/standard-telephonique-ia), [CloudTalk FR](https://www.cloudtalk.io/fr/blog/combien-coute-un-service-de-secretariat-telephonique-ia/)
- French custom voice integrations are quoted at EUR 6,000-15,000 HT setup plus EUR 0.10-0.25/min by one agency blog; an in-house receptionist is estimated at ~EUR 2,400 loaded per month (secondary) — [blckalpaca](https://blckalpaca.at/fr/blog/prix-dun-agent-vocal-ia-le-vrai-cout-dun-bot-telephonique-en-pme)
- French SME AI adoption is rising fast: France Num 2026 barometer (9,000 firms) reports 40% of TPE/PME use an AI solution, up from 26% in 2025 and 13% in 2024 — [Blog du Modérateur](https://www.blogdumoderateur.com/ia-cyber-budgets-10-chiffres-numerique-tpe-pme-2026/)
- French e-invoicing timetable creates a dated need (reception 2026-09-01, SME emission 2027-09-01) — [impots.gouv.fr](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/foire-aux-questions-je-decouvre-la-facturation-electronique)
- Morocco: Maroc PME launched the PACTE TPME 2026-2030 programme in April 2026, aimed at digitalisation and operational performance — [TelQuel](https://telquel.ma/2026/09/22/tribune-ia-la-prochaine-fracture-entre-les-entreprises-marocaines-sera-t-elle-organisationnelle_2008854)
- Morocco: a Kaspersky study presented at GITEX Africa 2026 says 75% of respondents use AI without a clear framework (methodology not checked) — [Médias24](https://medias24.com/2026/04/08/ia-en-entreprise-kaspersky-alerte-sur-des-usages-massifs-et-peu-encadres-au-maroc-1656067/)
- Morocco: cash is still 63% of transactions, per the "Value of Acceptance" study — a constraint on card-billed subscriptions — [La Vie Éco](https://www.lavieeco.com/argent/le-paiement-digital-un-catalyseur-de-croissance-pour-les-tpme/)
- Moroccan law: the CNDP states direct prospecting cannot be carried out without prior consent of the persons concerned (Law 09-08, art. 10) — [CNDP prospecting sheet, 2025](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/fiche_CNDP_Prospection_RT_fr.pdf)

### Inferences
- The legal asymmetry matters for outbound models: cold email aimed at Moroccan contacts looks consent-based under Law 09-08, so an M3-style service would have to target EU/US prospects under those regimes instead, or use opt-in lists in Morocco.
- Selling to French SMEs from Morocco in French, at prices below the EUR 79-99 anchors, is the most evidenced francophone route; selling to Moroccan SMEs faces lower purchasing power and payment friction.
- Candidate gaps worth validating, none proven: Darija-capable voice agents; tender monitoring for Moroccan (marchespublics.gov.ma) and West African portals; French-Arabic localization; pre-accounting packs for French micro-businesses ahead of 2027.

### Gaps
- No Moroccan or African barometer of SME AI adoption or willingness to pay was found; no pricing for any Morocco-based AI service operator.
- No data on Belgium or francophone sub-Saharan Africa.
- Whether GDPR-style "legitimate interest" B2B prospecting has any equivalent under Law 09-08 was not confirmed; needs a lawyer or the law's text.
- Moroccan foreign-exchange, invoicing and tax rules for selling abroad (auto-entrepreneur ceilings, Stripe availability) were outside what I researched.

## Key Question 5 — Evidence on churn and failure of "AI automation agencies" and AI wrappers

### Takeaway
The best-documented case is 11x: TechCrunch reported employees saying 70-80% of customers left, usually via three-month break clauses, against the company's claimed 79% retention. Beyond that, there is no independent dataset on AI-agency churn; the widely repeated failure percentages trace back to vendors, course sellers or studies of something else.

### Cited Findings
- 11x (TechCrunch, 2025-03-24): an employee said the company was "losing 70-80% of customers that came through the door"; one employee said it might report USD 14M ARR when contracts surviving the three-month trial totalled about USD 3M; 11x replied that its highest churn was in late-2023 cohorts and retention is 79%, and that the board saw contracted ARR — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/24/a16z-and-benchmark-backed-11x-has-been-claiming-customers-it-doesnt-have)
- Category-wide AI SDR annual churn of 50-70% is asserted by comparison blogs without a traceable dataset — [MarketBetter](https://marketbetter.ai/blog/ai-sdr-pricing-real-cost-2026/)
- Gartner (2025-06-25) predicts over 40% of agentic-AI projects will be cancelled by end of 2027, citing cost, unclear value and weak risk controls — this concerns enterprise projects, not agencies — [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-predicts-over-40-percent-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-canceled-by-end-of-2027)
- A vendor blog attributes to RAND that more than 80% of AI projects fail, about twice the rate of non-AI IT projects (I did not open the RAND source) — [Ciela](https://ciela.ai/blogs/why-ai-automation-agencies-fail-data)
- A vendor blog claims ~70% of automation-client churn happens in the first 90 days (single source, unverified) — [Ciela](https://ciela.ai/blogs/why-ai-automation-clients-churn-and-how-to-keep-them)
- Non-AI benchmark: retainer marketing agencies ~18% annual client churn, 32% for agencies under USD 1M revenue — [Focus Digital](https://focus-digital.co/average-marketing-agency-churn/)
- An unverified author claims that of 100 AI service businesses launched 2024-2026, 60 reached zero revenue, 30 reached USD 500-5,000/mo, 10 reached USD 10K+/mo — no dataset behind it — [dev.to](https://dev.to/lw_y_cc0e8114599c56bc1b44/ai-saas-is-dead-for-solo-founders-long-live-ai-services-53cl)
- Wrapper volatility in operator-connected data: SEObot's MRR shows -19.7% over 30 days — [first-mrr.com](https://first-mrr.com/study/seobot)
- Supplier dependence: Synthflow removed self-serve and per-minute plans in favour of annual enterprise contracts (state of page on 2026-11-11) — [Synthflow](https://synthflow.ai/pricing)

### Inferences
- The recurring pattern across the evidence is that services promising an *outcome the buyer can measure quickly* (booked meetings) churn hardest when the outcome fails to appear; services replacing a clear cost (missed calls, manual entry) have a more defensible retention story, though I have no churn data to confirm it.
- For an owner who does not want to operate daily, custom-build agencies (M23) are the worst fit: project revenue, bespoke maintenance, and the churn evidence all point the same way.
- Realistic planning should assume high early churn and treat any "passive" claim sceptically; the sourced examples of durable revenue all involve active product and distribution work.

### Gaps
- No independent, methodologically sound churn or survival dataset exists (that I could find) for AI automation agencies or AI wrappers. Every percentage in circulation should be labelled unverified.
- The "95% of AI agencies fail" figure has no traceable source and should not be used.
- No post-2025 follow-up on 11x's actual retention was found.
